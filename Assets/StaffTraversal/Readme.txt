STAFF Tangent Traversal

Controls: Hold Q to lock camera rotation while the camera continues to follow character translation. Move the mouse to steer a screen-space aiming reticle independently of the camera; the casting staff aims at that reticle. Release Q to launch at the highlighted building surface. At a wall, hold Q and release toward the next building without touching the ground. Space pushes off. Wall hold lasts 3 seconds. Escape, reset, character changes, blocked input and lost focus cancel the ability.

The tangent is WHITE and uses exactly TWO LineRenderer vertices. There is no rope sag, spline, or curved interpolation. All traversal effects and the valid aim reticle are white. No function curve is displayed at the target. The equation texture is generated by the actual project LaTeX toolchain; source and provenance live in Mathematics/Tangent.

Shared installation: STAFF Gameplay.prefab owns TangentTraversal on AdventurePlayer. AdventureCamera adds a smoothed FOV boost. Existing city, subway and SampleScene prefab instances inherit the ability. For another scene, use the shared gameplay prefab and add TangentSurface to the parent of colliders that should accept grappling. Unmarked objects occlude targeting but cannot be grappled. Inspector fields configure range, speed, wall hold duration, mask and clips. Existing Shift/RMB dash remains unchanged.

Selected Mixamo motions after visual sampling on Kafka_NoCoat at four phases each:
- Cast: Standing 1H Magic Attack 01 [128670910]
- Launch: Start Swinging [119840901], late launch segment
- Flight: Swinging [119830901], normalized 0.65-0.85 crouched segment
- Wall: Swing Into Wall [120480901], normalized 0.85
- Landing: Swing To Land [120470901], late landing segment
Flying [111080901] was rejected for its prone silhouette. Jumping Up and Falling To Roll were also compared. Existing library FBX import settings were preserved.

Movement is owned by the capsule controller; clips do not move the gameplay root. Baked hip displacement is recentered, and pose transitions blend over 0.08 seconds. A procedural casting staff attaches to the active humanoid hand. Non-humanoid characters retain traversal with their existing pose. Surface contact can stop at nearby protruding facade geometry instead of penetrating a balcony. The current landing uses wall contact or rooftop crouch; it does not perform an automatic ledge mantle.

Verification: Tools/STAFF/Install Tangent Traversal installs prefab references and city collision surfaces. Staff.Traversal.Editor.TangentVerification.Run runs actual Unity Play-mode traversal checks; results and camera renders are in Library/TangentReview. TangentAudition.Run renders the eight compared Mixamo clips at four phases.

City target coverage includes shared subway car hulls and animated doors, elevated railway undersides/supports, streets, metro entrance, and distant skyline. Shared range is 400m. Moving contacts retain local coordinates on their collider during flight and carry the character while attached; normal subway passenger carry is suppressed while traversal owns movement. TangentWorldVerification checks actual scene raycasts and moving anchors in Play mode.

Equation overlay: y=f'(a)(x-a)+f(a). The aiming vertical plane uses horizontal distance from the casting source as x; a is the horizontal source-to-contact distance, f(a) is target world Y, and f'(a) is signed tan(elevation angle). Values are displayed to two decimal places; vertical rays use x=a. Numeric substitution uses 61 official manimlib FadeIn samples (0.45s) and actual LaTeX glyph textures. The overlay is screen-space and cannot be occluded by buildings; contact ring remains in world space on its contact surface, with distance-based minimum size compensation. Camera aim release blends over 0.25 seconds. Generation source: Tools/export_tangent_math.py.

During Q aiming and the casting windup only the symbolic equation is shown. Numeric substitution starts on entry to Flying. Space immediately cancels Casting/Flying/Landing; at Wall it also pushes away.

Single equation row: symbolic terms fade into substituted values in place, with no second row. Negative target height uses subtraction, not a plus followed by minus; values rounding to zero display unsigned 0.00.

Equation display preserves the original 3m by 0.5m projected size and 6:1 box ratio. Only distant labels are clamped to an 24px minimum height. Numeric glyph layout uses LaTeX SVG bounds and shared baseline; no per-character height fitting.

Only the equation is rendered on a Screen Space Overlay Canvas (minimum 144x24 pixels, 6:1 aspect). The contact ring remains a world-space LineRenderer.

Space cancel applies an upward launch speed of 10 m/s (cancelJumpSpeed in the shared traversal component), including mid-flight cancellation. Wall cancellation also retains the 6 m/s outward push. Other cancellation paths do not add upward velocity.
