STAFF Tangent Traversal

Controls: Hold Q to freeze the camera position, rotation and field of view. Move the mouse to steer a screen-space aiming reticle independently of the camera; the casting staff aims at that reticle. Release Q to launch at the highlighted building surface. At a wall, hold Q and release toward the next building without touching the ground. Space pushes off. Wall hold lasts 3 seconds. Escape, reset, character changes, blocked input and lost focus cancel the ability.

The tangent is WHITE and uses exactly TWO LineRenderer vertices. There is no rope sag, spline, or curved interpolation. All traversal effects and the valid aim reticle are white. The separate white parabola depicts the local function whose tangent is being constructed. The equation texture is generated by the actual project LaTeX toolchain; source and provenance live in Mathematics/Tangent.

Shared installation: STAFF Gameplay.prefab owns TangentTraversal on AdventurePlayer. AdventureCamera adds a smoothed FOV boost. Existing city, subway and SampleScene prefab instances inherit the ability. For another scene, use the shared gameplay prefab and add TangentSurface to the parent of static colliders that should accept grappling. Unmarked objects occlude targeting but cannot be grappled. Inspector fields configure range, speed, wall hold duration, mask and clips. Existing Shift/RMB dash remains unchanged.

Selected Mixamo motions after visual sampling on Kafka_NoCoat at four phases each:
- Cast: Standing 1H Magic Attack 01 [128670910]
- Launch: Start Swinging [119840901], late launch segment
- Flight: Swinging [119830901], normalized 0.65-0.85 crouched segment
- Wall: Swing Into Wall [120480901], normalized 0.85
- Landing: Swing To Land [120470901], late landing segment
Flying [111080901] was rejected for its prone silhouette. Jumping Up and Falling To Roll were also compared. Existing library FBX import settings were preserved.

Movement is owned by the capsule controller; clips do not move the gameplay root. Baked hip displacement is recentered, and pose transitions blend over 0.08 seconds. A procedural casting staff attaches to the active humanoid hand. Non-humanoid characters retain traversal with their existing pose. Surface contact can stop at nearby protruding facade geometry instead of penetrating a balcony. The current landing uses wall contact or rooftop crouch; it does not perform an automatic ledge mantle.
