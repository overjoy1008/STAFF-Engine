Original renderer, shaders, noise textures, volume controls and editor UI are included.
STAFF changes: safe Create before VolumeManager initialization; optional camera filter; per-camera Nishita sun direction/color and ambient floor. The built-in directional lights are not modified. Temporal accumulation is disabled in the STAFF profile to avoid cross-camera history artifacts. Profile renders at half resolution, 64 primary steps and 4 lighting steps. Clouds use global sky mode; original upstream limitation: no orthographic cameras.
